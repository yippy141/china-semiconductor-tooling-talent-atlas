# Demo script

## One-minute version for DGA or ASG

I built a public-source prototype for tracking China's semiconductor-equipment workforce. The idea is simple: product announcements do not tell us whether Chinese toolmakers can support tools in real fabs. The better question is whether firms are building R&D teams, field application roles, service functions, calibration routines, training systems, and customer-support capacity.

The site starts with a short brief, then lets a reader click into firm dossiers, segment pages, an evidence explorer, and a source ledger. It uses public records only. It does not rank Chinese capability. It shows what the public record can support and where the most important knowledge remains hidden.

The client use case is competitor monitoring. A toolmaker, investor, or policy team can use this to track which Chinese firms are adding product breadth, which segments have source-checked records, and which indicators would deserve closer human research.

## Three-minute version

Start on the homepage.

Say:

This project asks whether China can staff its semiconductor-equipment push. That is a different question from whether China can announce tools or spend money. In chip tooling, the hard work is often customer-site support: chamber matching, calibration, field application, process tuning, maintenance recovery, and repeat deployment.

Scroll to the discipline matrix.

Say:

The first problem is that broad STEM counts are too blunt. Etch needs plasma and surface chemistry. Deposition needs thin-film chemistry, vacuum behavior, gas delivery, and chamber matching. Metrology needs optics, algorithms, calibration, and field support. Lithography sits in a different opto-mechatronic stack.

Scroll to firm workforce chart.

Say:

The strongest public window is not a university list. It is listed-company filings. AMEC, ACM Research Shanghai, and NAURA disclose workforce categories that show R&D scale, technical staff, advanced-degree staff, or service functions. These are firm-level figures, but they are much more useful than a generic graduate count.

Click a firm page.

Say:

The next click is the firm dossier. Here the reader sees what the firm does, which segment it touches, what the public record says, and what not to infer. The page links back into the explorer and source ledger.

Open explorer.

Say:

The explorer is the audit layer. It is for analysts who want to inspect rows, not for the first read. Rows are tagged by evidence type, confidence, segment, city, and source.

Close with:

The product is not a final intelligence platform. It is a working prototype for a client-facing monitoring product. The framework can extend to advanced packaging, critical minerals processing, power equipment, or other China critical-tech bottlenecks once the first domain is proven.
