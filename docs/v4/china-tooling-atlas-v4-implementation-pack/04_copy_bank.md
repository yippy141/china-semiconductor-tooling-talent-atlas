## Analyst voice section

Add this to methodology or the essay:

```text
I read Chinese public sources as records of visibility, not ground truth. A local shortage list tells me what a city wants to attract or subsidize. A product page tells me how a firm wants its capabilities understood. A filing is stronger, but even filings compress roles that matter in practice: applications engineering, field service, customer ramp, and production recovery. This monitor is built around that tension.
```

## Boss demo opening

```text
I built this as a prototype for monitoring China’s semiconductor-equipment ecosystem through the talent layer. The point is not to claim we can measure capability directly. The point is to track the public records that show whether Chinese toolmakers are building the R&D, service, application, and training structures that help turn product claims into production support.
```
