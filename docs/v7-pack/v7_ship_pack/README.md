# V7 Ship Pack — China Semiconductor Tooling Talent Atlas

This pack turns the current V6/V6.5 site into a shippable executive brief.

The decision: ship the full editorial hierarchy pass, not just a visual facelift. The site should open as a brief, then send readers into firm dossiers, segment briefs, the explorer, and the source ledger.

Core product promise:

> A public-source monitor for whether Chinese semiconductor-equipment firms are building the R&D, service, field-application, calibration, and training capacity needed to turn product claims into working fab tools.

Use these files in order:

1. `00_take_stock.md` — what is working and what still fails.
2. `01_v7_decisions.md` — final choices, including where I disagree with the Claude Design pack.
3. `02_two_day_demo_sprint.md` — what to finish before showing friends/advisors.
4. `03_one_week_ship_plan.md` — what to finish before showing DGA or potential employers.
5. `04_coding_agent_prompts.md` — prompts to paste into Claude Code or Codex.
6. `05_copy_bank.md` — approved copy and banned replacements.
7. `06_qa_checklist.md` — manual QA and review checklist.
8. `07_demo_script.md` — how to present the project in 2 minutes and 7 minutes.
9. `08_after_ship_roadmap.md` — what comes next after V7 ships.

Rules for this sprint:

- No new research unless a claim is visibly broken.
- No new sector modules.
- No Three.js command center.
- No capability score.
- No Western hard-number comparisons unless source-checked.
- No new data tables unless they are editorial metadata needed for display.
- Make one small commit after each prompt.
